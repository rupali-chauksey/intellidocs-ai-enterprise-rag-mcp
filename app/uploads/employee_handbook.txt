CHAPTER 2: ONBOARDING
=======================================================

2.1 First Day Checklist
- Collect your access card from reception (Ground Floor, Block A)
- Meet your HR buddy who will be assigned before joining
- Set up your laptop — IT support at extension 100 or it@techvision.in
- Complete online onboarding modules on the Learning Portal
- Attend the New Joiner Orientation at 10:00 AM in Conference Room B

2.2 First Week Activities
Day 1: HR orientation, office tour, laptop setup
Day 2: Department introduction, meet your team
Day 3: Product and services overview with the Sales team
Day 4: Systems and tools training with IT
Day 5: Meet your manager, discuss 30-60-90 day goals

2.3 Probation Period
All new employees serve a 3-month probation period.
Performance is reviewed at the end of month 1 and month 3.
Confirmation of employment is issued after successful completion of probation.
During probation, notice period is 2 weeks on either side.

2.4 Required Documents
Please submit these to HR within your first 3 days:
- Aadhar card (original + photocopy)
- PAN card
- Previous employer experience letter and relieving letter
- Last 3 months salary slips
- Bank account details for salary processing
- Educational certificates (originals for verification)

=======================================================
CHAPTER 3: CODE OF CONDUCT
=======================================================

3.1 Professional Behaviour
Treat all colleagues, clients, and vendors with respect and professionalism.
Discrimination of any kind based on gender, religion, caste, age or any other
protected characteristic is strictly prohibited and grounds for immediate termination.
Sexual harassment complaints are handled under the POSH Act 2013.

3.2 Confidentiality
All company information, client data, and business strategies are confidential.
Do not share company information with external parties without explicit approval.
Sign the Non-Disclosure Agreement on your first day.
Confidentiality obligations continue for 2 years after leaving the company.

3.3 Conflict of Interest
Inform HR immediately if you have a financial interest in a competitor or client.
Do not accept gifts worth more than Rs 1,000 from clients or vendors.
Moonlighting (working for another company simultaneously) is not permitted.

3.4 Social Media Policy
Do not post confidential company information on social media.
You may mention your employer on LinkedIn but do not speak on behalf of the company.
Content that brings the company into disrepute is grounds for disciplinary action.

CHAPTER 4: BENEFITS
=======================================================

4.1 Health Insurance
Group health insurance of Rs 5,00,000 per employee per year.
Coverage extends to spouse and up to 2 children.
Cashless treatment at 500+ network hospitals across India.
Contact hr@techvision.in for the list of network hospitals.

4.2 Learning and Development
Annual L&D budget of Rs 25,000 per employee.
Access to all internal TechVision courses free of charge.
External certifications pre-approved by your manager are reimbursable.
Study leave of up to 5 days per year for approved certifications.

4.3 Internet and Mobile Allowance
Employees in Sales and Engineering receive Rs 1,000/month internet allowance.
Mobile allowance of Rs 500/month for client-facing roles.
Reimbursement submitted monthly through the expense portal.

4.4 Employee Referral Programme
Refer a candidate who gets hired and completes 3 months — receive Rs 15,000.
Referral bonus paid in the month following the referee's 3-month completion.
No limit on the number of referrals per employee.
