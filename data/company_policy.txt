Company Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 24 paid leave days per calendar year, accrued
monthly at 2 days per month. Unused leave up to 10 days can be carried
forward to the next year; any excess is forfeited on December 31st.

Sick leave is separate from paid leave: employees get 12 sick days per year,
which do not carry forward. A medical certificate is required for sick leave
exceeding 3 consecutive days.

Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed
quarterly.

Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date via
the internal expense portal, with itemized receipts attached. Expenses
above $500 require manager pre-approval.
